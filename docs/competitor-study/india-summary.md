# Pan-India Competitor Landscape — Summary

**Source file:** `docs/competitor-study/india-competitors.xlsx` (sheet: "India Competitors")
**Compiled:** 2026-07-29 · **Method:** live web search (Google/Firecrawl search + scrape) against company sites, Tracxn/Crunchbase/PitchBook/YourStory/Inc42 funding coverage, Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn, and wedding directories (WeddingSutra, WedMeGood vendor listings). Every row has a verified real URL and, where available, a confirmed HQ city sourced from LinkedIn/company "About" pages. No companies were invented, and none duplicate the 20 companies already covered in the Kerala-tier batch (WedMeGood, ShaadiSaga, WeddingWire India, WeddingBazaar, itsmy.wedding, Bliss, SitesPlaced, Wedd.ai, DesiEvite, Rayon Invitations, InviteCrafter, Townscript, Unicorn, Rainmaker, Melodia, SANS, Expressario, Panigrahana, Kalamaya, Gaala, Click Me Happy).

## Totals

- **23 companies** found and verified — within the requested 20–30 range, prioritizing breadth and verifiability over padding with weak/unconfirmed leads.
- **21 of 23** are India-headquartered (confirmed city for 19; 2 have no publicly listed city but are confirmed India-based via other signals — WhatsApp/phone numbers, "India" copy, founder LinkedIn location).
- **2 of 23** (Zoho Backstage, and arguably ShowRunner's namesake-confusion aside) are India-origin but enterprise/corporate-event-oriented rather than wedding-first — included because they are real India-built products that compete in the same "event creation/management" feature category Evenzi occupies.
- Geographic spread is much wider than the Kerala batch: **Bengaluru (6)**, **Ahmedabad (3)**, **Mumbai (2)**, **Chennai (2)**, plus one each in Gurugram, Kolkata, Jaipur, Lucknow, Delhi NCR, Surat, and Bangalore-adjacent Chamrajpet — vs. the Kerala batch's near-total concentration in Kochi/Ernakulam and Thrissur.

## Funding / scale patterns

This batch surfaced **real capital**, unlike the Kerala batch where funding data was almost entirely absent:

- **Meragi** — $9.1M Series A (2024), led by **Accel** and **Peak XV Partners**, with **Anupam Mittal** (Shaadi.com founder) participating. Founded 2021, Bengaluru. The best-funded company found across both batches.
- **The Wedding Company** — ₹8 Cr pre-seed (LVX, Tremis Capital) followed by a **$2.75M seed round** (2026, led by Wellingdon Advisors). Founded 2022, Bengaluru. Targets the ₹10L–₹50L wedding budget segment specifically.
- **VenueMonk** — Series A, ~$3.07M total raised (incl. Indian Angel Network), founded 2014 — one of the oldest companies in this batch, proof the venue-booking-marketplace model has multi-year staying power in India.
- **Event Planet** — $250K angel round (2026) from a US-based angel investor, at a ~₹30 Cr valuation. Founded 2024, Lucknow — notable as a non-metro (Tier-2 city) origin story.
- **Samaaro** — Bengaluru, founded 2020, no funding amount surfaced but positioned as an enterprise SaaS competing with Cvent/Bizzabo; corporate B2B, not wedding-consumer.

Contrast with Kerala: the Kerala-tier summary noted pricing was opaque for ~80% of companies and **no funded company was identified**. Here, 5 of 23 (22%) have a publicly reported funding event — evidence that pan-India wedding/event tech is meaningfully more venture-backed than the Kerala-specific market, concentrated almost entirely in Bengaluru-headquartered companies (Meragi, The Wedding Company, Samaaro all Bengaluru).

## Notable patterns

1. **Bengaluru is the clear hub**, not just for generic Indian tech but specifically for wedding/event tech: Meragi, The Wedding Company, Samaaro, Explara, and Plan a Wedding are all Bengaluru-headquartered — 5 of the 6 Bengaluru entries in this list. Ahmedabad is a secondary cluster (Wowsly + WeTales, same founder — Nilesh Tripathi runs both) plus a third Ahmedabad-adjacent invite boutique.
2. **A wedding/event tech "twin-brand" pattern exists**: WeTales.in (digital invitations) and Wowsly.com (broader event platform) are both founded and run by the same person (Nilesh Tripathi, Ahmedabad) — a single operator running a narrow product and a platform-play side by side. Worth watching as a template.
3. **Only one true full-suite competitor surfaced: iWed.ai.** Like itsmy.wedding in the Kerala batch, it is the single company whose feature set most closely mirrors Evenzi's roadmap (website builder + guest list/RSVP + digital invites + budget/checklist + guest photo gallery), and it is explicitly marketing itself as "India's first AI wedding platform." It deserves the same close, ongoing monitoring the Kerala summary recommended for itsmy.wedding.
4. **Digital invitations remains the most commoditized, most crowded sub-category** — 8 of 23 companies here (Celebrare, WeTales, Love Solutions, IndianWeddingCard.com, POSH Invites, WishNWed, Invitationswala, Samaro.ai) compete narrowly on invitation design/delivery, mirroring the Kerala finding almost exactly. Pricing where visible is low and per-invite (₹299–₹2,499), reinforcing the Kerala summary's "race-to-the-bottom, high-volume" read on this sub-category nationally, not just regionally.
5. **Venue/vendor marketplaces are a distinct and larger-scale category than in Kerala** — Weddingz.in (Times Internet-backed, 50,000+ events), VenueMonk (Series A, 40+ cities), Meragi ($9.1M Series A), and Event Planet ($250K angel) all monetize by connecting couples to venues/vendors rather than selling planning software directly. This category barely existed in the Kerala-tier list (no comparable marketplace found there) — it appears to be where pan-India venture capital is actually flowing, more than into planning-tool SaaS.
6. **B2B "sell-to-the-planner" tools are a second distinct lane not seen in Kerala at all**: ShowRunner, EventKroot, Samaaro, and Zoho Backstage all sell event-management software *to event companies/marketers*, not to couples directly. Evenzi is couple/host-facing, so these aren't direct competitors, but they signal that Indian event professionals are actively buying event-software subscriptions — a proof point that a paid tier for Evenzi's own host-facing tools is a viable model.
7. **Guest photo/media gallery is thin but real and AI-forward**: Kwikpic (Kolkata) and Samaro.ai both lead with AI facial-recognition photo sorting/sharing rather than a plain upload gallery — more technically sophisticated than the one Kerala-tier gallery product found (Click Me Happy, a photographer-booking marketplace with basic per-shoot galleries). If Evenzi builds out Media & Memories, AI face-matching for guest photo delivery is table stakes nationally, not a differentiator.
8. **Pricing transparency is still the exception, but slightly better than Kerala.** Love Solutions publishes a full itemized rate card (₹1,999–₹36,500 across invites/websites/packages) and WishNWed publishes a starting price (₹299) with an active discount code — genuinely rare, concrete pricing data useful for direct sales benchmarking. Most others (14 of 23) are quote-on-request, consistent with the Kerala finding that ~80% of this market requires a discovery call before price is disclosed.

## Coverage gaps / caveats for the sales team

- **ShowRunner (showrunner.in)** has no confirmed HQ city — searches surfaced a same-named but unrelated US media-industry product ("Asperii ShowRunner," built on Salesforce, for TV/production companies) that must not be confused with the Indian event-CRM product of the same name at showrunner.in.
- **Celebrare, iWed.ai, Samaro.ai, Love Solutions, and WishNWed** have no publicly confirmed HQ city despite being clearly India-based (WhatsApp numbers, "India"/"Indian couples" copy, .in domains, or founder LinkedIn profiles) — outreach should confirm city directly rather than assuming from the website.
- **Invitationswala (Surat)** has no standalone company website found — only an Instagram business account — so outreach must go via Instagram DM, the same pattern noted for several boutique Kerala invite designers in the prior batch.
- Several foreign-HQ platforms with active India operations were deliberately **excluded** to keep this batch India-origin-first per the brief: Ticket Fairy (LA-HQ, India ticketing ops via ticketfairy.in/@ticketfairyin), Eventdex (NJ-HQ, India-founder-adjacent), WhiteClover and ItsaYes.io (both Wedding-planning SaaS but Canada/Delaware-HQ). These are worth a follow-up "international platforms competing in India" pass if the sales team wants that lens too.
- A handful of promising leads could not be verified to a real, live company website within this pass and were dropped rather than guessed: "Invitation Maker" (Surat, per a Tracxn company-profile stub with no working site found), "Zohova," "WedAssist," and "GetTogether" (name collisions with unrelated non-Indian products, no verifiable Indian company found).
