Flight log · Chapter 01

How we took off.

They met the way the best stories start — unexpectedly, at work. Sreelekshmy had spent years in the sky as cabin crew before moving into a corporate role, and that’s where a certain aviation-obsessed operations enthusiast couldn’t stop finding reasons to talk to her. He knew every aircraft by its silhouette; she knew every airport by its 3 AM coffee. Somewhere between shared shifts and endless conversations about runways and layovers, work turned into something that felt a lot like home. Then she went back to flying, he stayed on the ground keeping operations running, and the distance only made them surer. Two people, two faiths, one very well-coordinated flight plan — cleared for forever on 26 January, with both our families on board.
